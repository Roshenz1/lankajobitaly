Step 1: Check Your Eligibility

Use the checker above to confirm you meet all requirements. The key requirement is that your residency in Italy is less than 6 years and you obtained your Sri Lankan license before getting Italian residency.

⏱️ Time: 5 minutes | 💰 Cost: Free

Step 2: Gather All Required Documents

Collect originals and make photocopies of all documents listed in the checklist above. Organize them in the order required by the embassy. Have documents certified/translated if necessary.

⏱️ Time: 1-2 weeks | 💰 Cost: €0-100 (translation/certification)

Step 3: Contact & Book Embassy Appointment

Sri Lankan Embassy in Rome: Via Ettore Ximenes, 8, Roma
Phone: +39 06 70453826
Email: slembassy.rome@mea.gov.lk
Sri Lankan Consulate in Milan: For residents in Lombardy and nearby regions

⏱️ Time: 1-3 weeks | 💰 Cost: Free

Step 4: Visit Embassy & Submit Application

Attend your appointment with all documents. Submit your application for license conversion verification. Pay the embassy fee (typically €50-150). Receive a reference receipt/confirmation letter.

⏱️ Time: 1 day | 💰 Cost: €50-150

Step 5: Apply at Motorizzazione/ACI/Autoscuola

With your embassy confirmation letter, visit your local Motorizzazione, ACI office, or registered Autoscuola. Submit your driving license conversion application. The embassy verifies with Sri Lanka DMT in the background.

⏱️ Time: 2-3 months | 💰 Cost: €20-100

Step 6: Receive Italian Driving License

After embassy verification is complete, you'll receive notification. Collect your official Italian driving license from Motorizzazione. Your Sri Lankan license is now converted.

⏱️ Time: 1-2 weeks after approval | 💰 Cost: €0

💡 Money-Saving Tips

  • Do the medical certificate (Form 4) at Motorizzazione directly - saves 50%
  • Check if your documents need translation before going to embassy
  • Ask embassy if they can process by post for non-Roman residents
  • ACI is often cheaper than private Autoscuolas for Motorizzazione processing

⚠️ Critical Warnings

🚫 Beware of Scams & Unauthorized Agents

  • No middlemen authorized: The Sri Lankan Embassy does NOT authorize any private agents or "professional services" to speed up the process
  • Agents will charge you 300-500€: To do what you can do for free yourself
  • They promise "fast-track": This doesn't exist - the process has a fixed timeline
  • Report scams: If you encounter unauthorized agents, report them to the embassy
  • Verify directly: Always contact the embassy directly; don't rely on intermediaries

⏰ Time-Sensitive Requirements

  • 6-Year Rule: If your Italian residency exceeds 6 years, you must take the full Italian driving exam (theory + practical)
  • License Validity: Your Sri Lankan license must still be valid (not expired)
  • Obtain License BEFORE Residency: If you got your Sri Lankan license AFTER getting Italian residency, you cannot use this conversion process
  • Act within the window: Apply while you're still within the 6-year window to get exam exemption

📋 Document Requirements

  • Original documents required: You must present originals, not just photocopies
  • Name discrepancies: If your name is different on different documents, bring birth certificates and name change documents
  • Expired passport: Some officials accept passports expired within 3 years; check with embassy first
  • Translations: Ask embassy if translations are needed BEFORE you go; don't assume

✅ ඔව්! ඔබ ශ්‍රී ලංකා බලපත්‍ර එක්ස්පයිර්ඩ්ට නොවී ඉතාලිය ගිම්හරණ කිරීම පිට ගිත හැක. නිල! බලපත්‍ර, විසඳුපත්‍ර සහ Permesso di Soggiorno සතුව තබා ගන්න. ඉතාලි බලපත්‍ර ලැබුවා නම්, ශ්‍රී ලංකා බලපත්‍ර ඉතාලිය ගිම්හරණ කිරීම සඳහා වලංගු නොවේ.

❌ අවසරය දිය යුතු. ඔබ දෙතුරැ පරීක්ෂණ දිය යුතු: ① ගැටලු පරීක්ෂණ (40 ප්‍රශ්න), ② ගිම්හරණ පරීක්ෂණ (තිරුම් දෙස). Autoscuola ට එකතු වුවිට සිතුවුවු: €600-1500. අඩු අවසරයෙන්!
My name is spelled differently on my license and passport - what do I do? ▼
Bring both documents to the embassy interview. If it's a minor spelling variation (Kumara vs Kumar), it usually isn't an issue. If there's a significant difference, get a certified birth certificate or a statement from Sri Lankan authorities showing the name change (if applicable). Contact the embassy beforehand to ask what they need.
Can I use an Autoscuola to apply for the conversion? ▼
Yes! Many Autoscuolas are registered with Motorizzazione and can handle the conversion paperwork for you. Cost is usually €30-100. This saves you a trip to Motorizzazione, but you still must visit the embassy yourself with original documents. Using an Autoscuola is recommended if you're in a region without a convenient Motorizzazione office.
Can I use an expired permesso di soggiorno? ▼
No. Your permesso must be valid. If it's expired, renew it before going to the embassy. The embassy needs to verify your residency timeline. Bring your current, valid permesso di soggiorno and Certificato di Residenza.
How long does the entire process take? ▼
Typically 3-6 months from start to finish: 1-2 weeks for document gathering, 1-3 weeks for embassy appointment, 1 day for embassy visit, then 2-3 months for Motorizzazione verification with Sri Lanka DMT, and finally 1-2 weeks to collect your license. The longest wait is the embassy-to-Motorizzazione-to-Sri Lanka verification process.
What if I haven't renewed my Sri Lankan passport? ▼
The embassy will likely ask to see your passport. An expired passport from the past 3 years may be accepted, but contact the embassy first to confirm. If it's expired beyond 3 years, renew it at the embassy before applying for license conversion. Passport renewal typically takes 1-2 weeks.
Can I do this process entirely by post? ▼
Partially. Some embassies accept postal submissions, but you need to confirm this with the embassy directly. Original documents must be sent (with certified copies), and you'll need a courier for security. It's generally safer to visit in person to ensure nothing is lost and to answer any questions on the spot.
Is there a "fast-track" or expedited process? ▼
No official fast-track exists. The process timeline is set by the Sri Lankan DMT and Italian Motorizzazione. Anyone claiming to offer "expedited" conversion is likely a scam. Ignore offers from unauthorized agents. The standard process is your only legitimate option.
What's the difference between Motorizzazione, ACI, and Autoscuola? ▼
Motorizzazione (Ufficio della Motorizzazione Civile): Government office that issues Italian licenses. ACI (Automobile Club d'Italia): Non-profit that offers services like tests and paperwork processing, partner with Motorizzazione. Autoscuola: Driving school that can submit paperwork on your behalf. For conversion, you can go directly to Motorizzazione (free) or use ACI/Autoscuola (costs €30-100 extra but saves time).
