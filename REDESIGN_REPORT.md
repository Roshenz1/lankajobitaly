- Sinhala is the primary language (60% of text)
- English is secondary and parenthetical

- Numbered sections (①②③④⑤) for easy scanning

- No jargon without explanation
- Complex concepts explained with examples
- Conversational tone, not official

- High contrast colors
- Emoji support for visual clarity
- Mobile-friendly design (already in place)

### 5. Community Feel
- Like getting advice from a friend
- Practical tips included
- Real examples with numbers
- Supportive tone (✅ when good, 🔴 when critical)
